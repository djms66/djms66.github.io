Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact

Write to contact@danielstreng.fr. To help us answer quickly, please mention your device (Mac, iPad or iPhone), the app version (Options › About) and, if possible, a screenshot of the problem. We reply within a few working days.

Frequently asked questions

What does each device do?

Mac and iPad have the full design tools. iPhone is a companion: review your sites, fix text, replace images, preview and publish.

Where are my pages stored?

On your device, in the app's Documents folder. If you turn on sync, they also go through the folder you choose (iCloud Drive or another service).

How do I put my site online?

Tap “Publish”. The first time, choose where your site lives: Netlify, your web host (FTP), your WordPress site or files to upload yourself. Atelier Web remembers your choice.

What is free, and what do I pay for?

Creating, editing and previewing are free and unlimited. Publishing requires the one-time purchase “Atelier Web Full”, valid on every device signed in with the same Apple Account, or the 7-day free trial. There is no subscription, and the trial never charges you when it ends. The price is shown on the App Store and in the app (Options › Atelier Web Full), in your country's currency.

I bought it on another device. How do I unlock it here?

Open Options › Atelier Web Full and tap “Restore Purchases”, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I use any image, font or piece of music?

Only what you have the right to use. The fonts, sounds and sample pieces included in the app are free to use (licences listed in Options › About). Imported scores must be in the public domain or yours.
