Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short

Atelier Web collects no personal data. No account, no usage statistics, no advertising, no tracking. The publisher receives nothing you create.

What stays on your device

  • Your pages, images, fonts and imported pieces (MIDI, MusicXML, ABC) are stored in the app's Documents folder, on your device.
  • Only the photos and files you choose yourself are read.
  • Your WordPress password and your Netlify or FTP credentials are kept encrypted in Apple's keychain and are only used to connect to those services.
  • A local timestamp helps calculate the 7-day trial and prevents changing the device clock from extending it. It stays on your device and is not sent to the publisher.

Connections made by the app

  • Only to the addresses you give it: your WordPress site, your hosting (Netlify, FTP) and the web pages you choose to import. Those sites receive the usual information of a visit (IP address, device type) under their own rules.
  • Instrument sounds for sheet music are included in the app. They are only fetched online (GitHub) as a fallback, if missing.
  • Sync between your devices goes through the folder you choose (iCloud Drive or another service), under that service's terms.

Purchases

The free trial and the full-version purchase are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The publisher receives neither your name, nor your address, nor your payment details.

Sites you publish

Sites made with Atelier Web contain no tracker added by the app. If you add forms or third-party services, you are responsible for them towards your visitors.

Children

The app collects no data, including children's data.

Your rights and contact

Since no data about you is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete at the publisher's. Any question: contact@danielstreng.fr.
